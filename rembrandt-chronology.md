## Talk Talking Points

- The Night Watch (SK-C-5, 1642) sits roughly two-thirds of the way through this 10-work sample and is the only work dated in the 1640s.
- The sample spans Rembrandt's early Leiden period (1626) through his late Amsterdam masterpieces (1660–1665), including The Syndics (SK-C-6, 1662) and The Jewish Bride (SK-C-216, 1665).
- The 18-year gap between 1642 and 1660 is the biggest jump in the collection sample: dated holdings in this sample are concentrated in the 1630s (4 works) and 1660s (4 works), with sparse representation in the 1620s (1) and 1640s (1).
